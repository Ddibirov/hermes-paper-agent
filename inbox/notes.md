# Notes — for the desk that resurfaces your own writing

Short lines, one per item. The model's value is choosing what to resurface.

- A note from last January: "resolved: take the stairs, not the lift, at work." Three months in, still climbing — worth a small "this week last year" line.
- Reminder set eight months ago: "ask about the crown, it has been a year." The dentist is next Tuesday.
- Journal entry, a year ago this week: the fennel came in with the first frost, and the plan to try again this autumn.
- A draft kept in notes: the good butcher on the corner is closing at the end of the month; wrote it and never sent it.
- Cleaning the study: found the photo from the beach the day Nell arrived, two summers back.
