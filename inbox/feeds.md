# Feeds — Friday's news, cut to a paragraph each

Each block is one item. The prose desks pick three to five and write one
story each; they must carry a `sources:` entry for anything they use.

## Regulator signals it may cap data-centre power draw

The grid operator floated capping how much power new data centres can draw
during peak hours in the region this winter. The proposed cap would pause new
connections on the busiest twenty afternoons. ISPs and the chamber oppose it;
the operator says it is a hedge against rolling outages.

https://example.com/power-cap

## Metro finishes Orange Line track work a day early

Single-tracking between Stadium–Armory and Eastern Market ended last night,
a day ahead of schedule. Normal service resumes this morning; the shuttle
buses are being stood down.

https://wmata.example/advisories/orange

## City council votes the bike-lane network tonight

Final reading on the east-side connector, the last segment of the planned
through-route. The vote is expected to pass along party lines; construction
would start in spring.

https://example.com/bikelanes

## A warmer, wetter week ahead

Highs near ninety from Tuesday, with storms each afternoon after three. The
long-range signal favours a wetter-than-usual October across the mid-Atlantic.

## National Gallery opens the late Rothko room

The permanent collection will keep the renovated east room to Rothko's late
works from the eleventh, timed to the anniversary of his birth. Free, first
come first served at the entrance.
