Single-tracking between Stadium–Armory and Eastern Market ended last night, a
day ahead of the posted schedule. Normal service resumes this morning and the
shuttle buses are being stood down.

This is the segment that has been adding a quarter of an hour to anything
east of the Capitol since the work began, and it is the segment between you
and the dentist this afternoon. The note you left on that appointment said to
walk it because the line was up; as of this morning that is true again, and
the walk is now a choice rather than the only option.

Track work of this kind usually finishes late. This one finished early, which
is worth recording for the next time an advisory says a fortnight and you are
deciding whether to believe it.
