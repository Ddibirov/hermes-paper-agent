A journal entry from this week last year: the fennel came in with the first
frost, and the plan was to try again this autumn. The frost is a long way off
— the week ahead does not drop below 64°F — but the seed order is the sort of
thing that wants doing before the weather makes it obvious.

Two other lines have been waiting in the same file. The good butcher on the
corner is closing at the end of the month; you wrote a draft about it and
never sent it, and the end of the month is now three weeks out. And clearing
the study turned up the photograph from the beach, the day Nell arrived, two
summers back — which is a useful thing to have found in the week she arrives
again, on Saturday, at eleven.

None of this is urgent. That is rather the point of keeping it: the paper
surfaces it, and what happens next is yours.
