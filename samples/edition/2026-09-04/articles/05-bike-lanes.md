The council takes its final reading tonight on the east-side connector, the
last unbuilt segment of the planned through-route. The vote is expected to
pass along party lines, and construction would start in spring.

The connector matters more than its length suggests: it is the piece that
turns three disconnected stretches into one continuous route, which is the
difference between a lane you use for part of a trip and a lane you plan a
trip around. Nothing changes tomorrow either way — spring is two seasons off,
and a spring start in this city has meant summer before now.
