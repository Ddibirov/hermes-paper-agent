The permanent collection gives its renovated east room to Rothko's late works
from the eleventh, timed to the anniversary of his birth. Entry is free and
first come, first served at the door.

The late works are the dark ones, and they are the ones that ask for a room
rather than a wall. A renovated space and no ticket is a good combination for
a weekday morning when the calendar is clear — and the eleventh is a Thursday,
which is exactly such a morning.
