Four fixed points between now and Tuesday, and one of them is a reminder
rather than an appointment.

| When | What |
|:---|:---|
| Fri 09:30 | Henley review, on a call |
| Fri 14:30 | Dentist, cleaning |
| Sat 11:00 | Nell arrives by train |
| Sun 13:00 | Commanders at home |
| Tue | Ask about the crown |

The weekend is the clear part of the week — 87°F on Saturday and 83°F on
Sunday, both dry — so anything that needs good weather should go there rather
than wait. Tuesday's line is the one that has moved before: it was set eight
months ago, it has been carried forward since, and it is on the list again
because the paper does not quietly drop the things you asked it to remember.
